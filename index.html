01_ABOUT

The person behind the pivot tables

A little context before you get to the work.

I started out reconciling spreadsheets for a regional retailer and got hooked the first time a messy sales report turned into a chart that made a VP change a pricing decision on the spot. That's the job, as far as I'm concerned: find the signal, make it undeniable, help someone act on it.

These days I work across the full pipeline — pulling and cleaning data with SQL and Python, modeling it, and packaging the result into dashboards and reports that non-technical stakeholders actually read. I care as much about how a number is presented as how it was calculated.

Outside of work I mentor early-career analysts, contribute to a couple of open-source dashboard templates, and I'm currently deep in a personal project mapping public transit efficiency in my city.
