About me

Who's behind the dashboards

I don't just work with data. I turn it into decisions.

I'm a data analyst currently working as a Research Analyst at Technoculture Building Centre in Patna, where I sit between raw operational systems and the people who have to make calls on them — sales, marketing, finance and management.

Most of my work is the unglamorous half of analytics done properly: collecting property, sales, customer and market data from scattered sources, cleaning and validating it, reconciling booking, payment and ledger records, then turning the result into MIS reports and Power BI dashboards that managers actually open.

I lean heavily on automation. Replacing recurring manual spreadsheets with automated reporting workflows has been the single highest-leverage thing I do — it has improved reporting efficiency and data accuracy by up to 20% and removed around 10 hours of manual work a week. Alongside the business reporting, I build analysis projects in Python, SQL and Excel to keep sharpening the analytical side.

How I work

Four steps, every time

01

Understand

Start with the business question, not the dataset. Who decides what, and what would change their mind.

02

Clean

Pull the data together, validate it, reconcile it against source records. Nothing gets analysed before it's trusted.

03

Analyse

Use SQL, Excel, Python and BI tools to find the trend, the outlier or the gap that actually matters.

04

Recommend

Turn the finding into a dashboard or a one-page report with a clear recommendation attached.
